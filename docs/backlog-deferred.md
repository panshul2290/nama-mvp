# Deferred backlog

None of these items are built. Each is **Deferred until tender go/no-go**: they appear in the prototype or the tender scope, but are out of scope for the working MVP.

| # | Item | What it would cover | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incident SLA screen | Incident list with P1–P4 priority, a response and resolution timer per priority, breach highlighting, escalation, and an audit of who acknowledged and resolved each incident and when | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
| 2 | Permit reject / resubmit | NWS reviewer can reject a permit application with reasons; the company fixes the documents and resubmits; every decision is recorded in an audit trail (who, when, what changed) | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
| 3 | Driver offline trip capture and POD rejection | Driver app keeps working without signal (queues GPS fixes, steps and photos, then syncs); proof of delivery can be rejected by the customer or NWS with a reason and a follow-up | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
| 4 | Customer failure states | Clear screens and recovery paths for payment failed, no tanker available, customer offline or poor connection, and low wallet balance | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
| 5 | B2B approver and finance roles | Business accounts with requester, approver and finance roles; approval limits; invoices and credit terms | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
| 6 | Full Arabic / RTL | Arabic translations for every screen, right-to-left layout, Arabic numerals where required, and bilingual notifications | **Deferred until tender go/no-go** |
